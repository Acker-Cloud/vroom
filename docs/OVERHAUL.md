# VROOM 13: the overhaul

VROOM stops being a toddler's toybox and becomes a loud, late-90s console style 3D racer: chunky
low-poly machines, riveted gunmetal menus, twelve dramatic worlds, monsters that lunge out of the
scenery and bosses that chase you to the flag. Same name, same 12 worlds and 120 levels, same saves.
New engine (three.js r170, built like PuttX), new physics, new cars, new everything on screen.

This file is the design and the module map. `src/contracts.js` is the binding version of every
interface below; when the two disagree, fix whichever is wrong in the same change. `SPEC.md`,
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` describe v13; v12 itself lives in `legacy/v12/` (game,
suites, art) as a reference.

## 1. Audience and rules

Players around 10 to 12, PuttX rules: harder, faster, real skill, nothing patronising.

Kept from v12, enforced by the suites:

- **Zero words on screen.** Numerals, symbols and pictograms only. `aria-label`s are fine. The one
  word is the VROOM logo on the splash and home screens, marked `data-logo` (as PuttX's title sign).
- **No fail states.** Crashes, monsters, bosses, gaps and walls knock you back, spin you out, dent the
  car and cost time. Nothing ends a run. Fall into a gap and a crane hook drops you back at the last
  checkpoint (about 2 s lost). Every race finishes; a boss always loses the race to the flag.
  Stars only go up: every best is kept, the wallet only grows from racing.
- **Tap targets at least 64 px rendered at 1024x768** (75 stage px; use 88 or more).
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`** turns off decorative motion: screen shake, logo glints, idle bobs,
  camera sway, menu parallax. Gameplay motion stays.
- **Save data is forever.** See section 9.
- **No em dashes** anywhere: code, comments, docs, commit messages.
- One shipped file, offline, no network: `index.html` is generated by `npm run build`.

Dropped: the Toybox look (`--v-*` cream, wood, putty, rounded kid chrome), the fuel and tyre chores and
the wash minigame (their save fields are kept and round-tripped, see section 9), lane-based steering.

**IP rule (hard).** VROOM is an original game in a late-90s console *style*. Nothing from Crash
Bandicoot, Naughty Dog or any other existing game: no characters, mascots, names, masks, fruit,
crate-with-symbol mechanics, level names, music, logos or look-alike art. Every creature, machine,
world and sound in this document is our own. Generic tropes (a sandworm-like creature, a yeti-like
brute) are fine only with an original design and an original name. Avoid real brand names too
(no real monster truck names).

hazard stripes, rust bleeding from bolt holes, molten light from below. The 3D is chunky low-poly,
flat or faceted shading, saturated dramatic skies, hard rim light, heavy fog colour.

- **Gunmetal plate**: the `--metal` gradient, 4 px steel rim, 4 corner rivets (radial-gradient dots),
  small radius (8 to 12 px, nothing bubbly), hard `0 6px 0` drop to `--m-soot` plus a soft dark blur.
- **Chrome**: 5-stop steel gradient with a moving glint band (off under reduced motion).
- **Rust and grime**: layered radial-gradients in rust and soot on plate edges and bolt holes,
  a scratched-steel hairline texture (repeating-linear-gradient at 1 to 2 percent opacity).
- **Hazard stripes**: on the GO plate's border, the boss health bar, locked-world gates.
- **Molten glow**: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px var(--acc2)` on selected and live things, never on chrome text.
- **Numerals**: heavy italic grotesque (`"Arial Black", Impact`), white with a black 3 px stroke and
  a hard drop, like stencilled race numbers. Stars are chunky 5-point gold with an orange rim.
- **Press feel**: every press is a 4 px slam down with a metallic clank (`sfx('press')`), no squash.
- **Transitions**: a hazard-striped shutter slams shut and opens (reduced motion: a 150 ms fade).

The **VROOM logo**: italic black wide letters, chrome face (the PuttX chrome gradient and glint), a
black cut-out outline, a 10-step extrusion stepping down into molten orange, cracked and welded
plates behind it with sparks, tilted -6 degrees. On the splash it slams in from the camera with a
shockwave and spark shower; on home it hangs over the world-of-the-day diorama.

### 3D art direction (owners cars, worlds, monsters)

- **Low-poly, big shapes**: 6 to 12 sided cylinders, chamfered boxes, no smooth subdivision. Silhouettes
  must read at 100 px tall. `flatShading: true` on MeshStandardMaterial; metalness and roughness per part.
- **Procedural only**: geometry built in code, textures drawn on canvases at load (rust, hazard, tread,
  grime, decals), cached in `ctx.cache`. No image files, no external models.
- **Lighting**: one strong key (the world's sun, moon or lava), a coloured hemisphere fill, fog in the
  sky colour. Rim light is cheated with an emissive edge or a back light per world.
- **Performance budget** (iPad at 60 fps, the race scene): under 150 draw calls, under 250k triangles,
  shadow map at most 1024 from the key light only (off when `ctx.quality === 'low'`), instanced meshes
  for repeated roadside pieces, merged static geometry per track chunk, no per-frame allocation.

## 4. Monsters

Two creature kinds per world plus a finale boss. Behaviour is deterministic (`src/monsters.sim.js`),
looks and animation are `src/monsters.js`. Archetypes (contracts `ARCHE`):

- **lurker**: waits beside the road, telegraphs (eyes glow, ground cracks, a roar) for `tell`
  seconds once the car is within `trigger` metres, then lunges across the lanes. Dodge or eat a knock.
- **roamer**: walks or runs along the road, weaving; overtake it cleanly.
- **swarm**: a pack of small ones crossing together; each hit is small.
- **diver**: from above, its shadow on the road first.
- **burrower**: a wake in the ground tracking toward the car, then erupts.
- **boss**: the finale chase on levels 10, 20 ... 120. It starts behind, rubber-bands to stay close
  and menacing, throws attacks telegraphed on the road (slams, thrown debris spawned as `debris`
  actors, sweeps), and is beaten by reaching the flag (`boss` event `defeat`). It never wins.

Hits: `KINDS[kind].knock` (impulse, lift, spin, stun) applied by the sim with momentum (heavier cars
and armor upgrades shrug more off). Never a stop longer than about 1 s.

## 5. Physics

`src/sim.js`, owner physics. DOM-free, THREE-free, deterministic, fixed step `SIM_DT = 1/120` s,
seeded rng in `SimState.rng`, bit-exact maths from `src/dmath.js` only, so it ports to Swift for iOS
exactly as PuttX's `// #region sim` does (same evaluation order, golden traces compared in tests).

### Track

`buildTrack(spec)` turns the authored pieces into a ribbon sampled every 1 m: centre, tangent, surface
up, right, half width, surface code, flags. Pieces (contracts `PIECES`): straight, bend, banked, hill,
crest, ramp, gap, corkscrew, loop, halfpipe, split, rumble. Walls line both edges unless a piece
says otherwise; gaps have no walls and no surface. Checkpoints every ~250 m; the finish line flag.

### Car model

A rigid body (mass, inertia from `CarStats.size`) on four raycast wheels:

- **Suspension**: per-wheel spring-damper along the body's down axis against the track surface
  (`suspK`, `suspC`, `suspTravel`, `rideH`), so the body pitches under braking, squats on throttle,
  rolls in corners and soaks up rumble.
- **Tyres**: longitudinal drive and brake force limited by a friction circle; lateral grip from slip
  angle with a peak and a falloff (`grip`, surface multiplier, wheel grip). Past the peak the car slides.
- **Drift**: hold drift (or flick steer at speed) to drop rear grip to `driftGrip`; counter-steer holds
  the angle; a clean drift banks boost (`driftBank` event). Spin-outs from oil, mines, hits.
- **Engine**: power-limited thrust with a top speed, a gear and rpm model for the engine sound
  (`rpm`, `gear` on CarState). Boost: a meter (0..1, tank upgrade = longer) that adds `boostPower`.
- **Air**: ballistic under world gravity, `airDrag`; air control rotates pitch and roll (`airControl`,
  springs upgrade); landing on the wheels is smooth, landing nose-first or on the roof bounces and
  rights itself (no fail). Flips, rolls and spins in the air count as tricks and bank boost.
- **Collisions**: walls (restitution, tangential scrape, a glance keeps speed, a head-on bleeds it),
  soft props fly off with momentum and cost a little speed, hard props bounce and spin, monsters by
  mass ratio and `knock`, pads and patches by category (contracts `PROP_CAT`).
- **Respawn**: below the track or stuck for 3 s: the crane drops the car at the last checkpoint.

### Tuning targets (the physics owner proves these in `tests/physics-check.cjs`)

| Quantity | Target |
|---|---|
| top speed, base monster truck | 40 m/s (144 km/h); racers 50; tanks 34; boost +30 percent |
| 0 to 30 m/s | 3.0 to 3.5 s base, 2.4 s with engine 3 |
| peak lateral grip on asphalt | 1.0 to 1.2 g; ice 0.15 g; oil 0.3 g; mud 0.6 g |
| braking 40 to 0 | under 3.5 s |
| ramp air | 1 to 3 s typical, 5 s mega ramps; orbit world 2.5x hang time |
| landing | a 3 s drop on wheels loses under 10 percent speed |
| wall glance at 15 degrees | keeps 85 percent of speed |
| monster knock | at most 1 s of lost control |
| race length | 45 to 120 s for a good run (levels grow through world 3, then get harder, not longer) |
| determinism | same (level, seed, inputs) gives identical bits in Node, Chromium, Safari and Swift |

Medals: `par.S/A/B` per level from `levels.js`, proven by the autopilot. Rating 1 to 3 from stars
collected. Fairness (worlds owner, `tests/fairness-check.cjs`): a skill-0.4 autopilot must finish every
level under 2x par with at most 3 respawns; skill 1 must reach par A. Fix content, never thresholds.

## 6. Scenes

`splash -> home -> garage | map -> race -> results`, each a `VROOM.scenes[id]` (contracts Scene API).

- **splash** (ui): black; a foundry hammer slams, sparks, the chrome VROOM logo smashes through a
  steel plate with a shockwave and an engine roar; about 2 s, any tap skips.
- **home** (ui): the world-of-the-day diorama behind; the logo; the player's machine idling in front,
  revving on tap; a huge hazard-rimmed GO plate (next level), garage, map, profile (avatar) plates;
  the wallet chip. An attract-mode loop may race the autopilot behind the menu after 20 s idle.
- **garage** (cars): section 3.
- **map** (ui): twelve world tabs as riveted plates with their emblems (`worlds.icon`), locked
  worlds behind a hazard gate; a world page is a route of 10 level plates over its backdrop, each
  with its number, 1 to 3 stars and medal; the finale plate shows the boss emblem.
- **race** (game): countdown (3, 2, 1 numerals and a GO light), HUD (ui): star count, timer, boost
  meter, checkpoint pips, boss distance bar on finales, position of the ghost (best replay). Controls
  (ui): on-screen gas, brake, boost, drift plates and a steering drag zone (or tilt, `progress.ctrl`);
  keyboard WASD / arrows, space boost, shift drift.
- **results** (ui): a slammed gunmetal card: time, stars, rating, medal stamp, payout tallying into
  the wallet, new-best flare; replay, garage, next.
- overlays (ui): profiles (3 avatars, QR save code, copy/paste code), trophy album (badges, bosses).

## 7. Audio

All synthesized (`src/audio.js`, owner ui), one master compressor, quiet mode. Music: driving
distorted-synth metal riffs per world (palm-muted saw chugs, a drum machine with gated snare, a
world-specific lead scale), a boss variant, a menu theme; all original. Engine: layered detuned saws
and a noise rumble keyed to `rpm`, `load`, boost whine, off-throttle crackle, per class voice. SFX:
contracts `SFX` list (metal clanks, crunches, skids, roars, slams).

## 8. FX

`src/fx.js`, owner worlds: sparks (wall scrapes, landings), dust and dirt plumes, tyre smoke and skid
marks, nitro flame, explosions (mines), splashes, debris (soft props), weather particles, screen shake
(game camera, off under reduced motion), hit-stop of 60 ms on big impacts. Pooled, no per-frame
allocation, capped by `ctx.quality`.

## 9. Save compatibility

Everything in `src/save.js` (owner integrator), ported from v12 sections 4 and 17. Proven by
`tests/save-check.cjs` against `tests/fixtures/v12-saves.json`, captured from the shipped v12.9 game.

- **Keys**: `vroom.v2.p<n>` per profile (3 profiles), `vroom.meta`; `vroom.v1` and single-profile
  `vroom.v2` still migrate. No key change for v13.
- **Catalog orders**: `contracts.LEGACY` holds the v12 orders exactly (bodies, wheels, colours,
  decals, buddies, extras, badges, code lists, prices). Live orders are LEGACY followed by
  `catalog.NEW_*` (append only, never reorder or remove). Every legacy id must stay in the catalog;
  v12 free parts stay free; v12 paid parts stay owned.
- **Compact codes** (`VROOM1.`, format 3, the QR): unchanged layout; lists are count-prefixed so
  appended ids decode in both directions. New fields go on the end of the v3 tail behind a
  `pos + n <= bytes.length * 8` guard. 13.0 appends 12 bits: bosses beaten per world.
- **Full codes** (`VROOM2.` gzip JSON, `VROOM3.` plain): the whole save minus replays, ghosts, gift stamp.
- **JSON fields**: every v12 field is loaded and written back. v13 adds `bosses` (worlds whose boss is
  beaten), `ctrl` ({ mode, sens }), and per level `t13` (best v13 time; the v12 `bestTime` is kept but
  never compared, the tracks differ) and `replay` ({ t, ticks, rle }: run-length packed inputs, a
  deterministic ghost). Unknown top-level fields round-trip untouched. `fuel`, `tread`, `muddy` and the
  v12 `ghost` traces are retired from play but kept.
- **Default build** for a fresh save: `monster` body, `monster` wheels, red (`#e53935`), all free.

Tests: each owner adds `tests/<owner>-check.cjs` (and the worlds owner `tests/fairness-check.cjs`);
`tests/run-all.cjs` finds them by name. Shared test files (`tests/lib.cjs`, `run-all.cjs`, `sim.cjs`,
`boot-check.cjs`, `save-check.cjs`, `build-check.cjs`, `lint.cjs`), `scripts/build.cjs`,
`package.json`, `eslint.config.js` and the docs belong to the integrator. Nobody edits another
owner's file; ask the integrator for a contract change instead.

The full interface is `MODULE_API` in `src/contracts.js`. In short:

- `catalog`: parts tables with prices and `CarStats`; `price(kind, id)`; `stats(build)`.
- `levels`: `WORLDS[w]` (gravity, airDrag, surf, monsters, boss); `level(n)` -> LevelSpec (pieces,
  props, stars, monsters, boss, par); `free(seed, k)`.
- `sim`: `buildTrack`, `frameAt`, `toWorld`, `carParams`, `newRace`, `step`, `quantize`, `packInput`,
  `unpackInput`, `autopilot`, `result`; events on `state.events`.
- `monsterAI`: `KINDS`, `spawn`, `think`, `onHit`; actors are kinematic, the sim owns contact.
- `save`: state, progress, ownership, economy (`buy`, `recordRun`), codes, profiles.
- `audio`: `sfx(name)`, `engine`, `music`, `ambience`, `unlock`, `setQuiet`.
- `fx`: `create(ctx, scene)` -> `{ emit, update, dispose }`.
- `cars`: `buildCar(ctx, build)` -> CarRig; `thumb`; the garage scene.
- `worlds`: `buildWorld(ctx, track, spec)` -> WorldRig; `backdrop(ctx, w)`; `accent(w)`; `icon(w)`.
- `monsters`: `build(ctx, kind)` -> MonsterRig.
- `ui`: `init`, `hud`, `controls`, `button`, `toast`, `transition`, `icons`; scenes splash home map results.
- `game`: `go`, `ctx`, `startRace`, `current`.

### Working in parallel

```bash
npm run build -- --out preview/<owner>.html             # your own preview, never index.html
npm run build -- --out preview/<owner>.html --mine <owner>   # others' files from git HEAD
python3 -m http.server 4173                             # then /preview/<owner>.html?scene=garage
node tests/lint.cjs --owner <owner>                     # your files only
node tests/sim.cjs                                      # the sim region in Node
npm test                                                # everything (builds preview/test.html)
```

URL switches: `?scene=<id>` (splash, home, garage, map, race, results, or a `dev:<owner>-<name>` scene
you register for previews), `?level=N`, `?bot=1` (autopilot drives), `?nosplash`, `?garage`,
`?quality=low`. Only the integrator writes `index.html` (`npm run build`) and commits it.
